Day 22–32: station receiving, discrepancy chain, TAT, exceptions, support.
Day 28–38: delivery and hanger loop.
Day 34–43: invoice, notifications, complaint CRM.
Day 40–50: admin operations, inventory, configuration.
Day 45–53: complete reports + security/audit hardening.
Day 50–56: integrated QA, controlled beta/UAT.
Day 57–60: release hardening and production readiness. No uncontrolled new features.
